01

WHY IS ZERO CALLED "LOVE"?

In tennis, the love means zero.
So a score of 15-love means one player
has won a point and the other has none.

02

40-40 ISN'T THE END!

At deuce, a player generally needs to
win two consecutive points to take the game
under standard advantage scoring.

03

THE BALL CAN CURVE!

Topspin makes a tennis ball rotate forward
and generally dip more sharply, while slice creates
backspin and a lower bounce.

04

A SHOT BETWEEN THE
LEGS HAS A NAME!

It's called a tweener-- also known as a hotdog.
It's a real shot, not just a trick seen in highlight videos.

05

TENNIS SCORES HAVE THREE LEVELS!

Points build games, games build sets, and sets
determine the match winner. Different competition formats
can change how the match is decided.
